Delete Your Account & Data — AI Study Scan Agent

Last updated: 1 July 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your AI Study Scan Agent account (app package com.aistudyscanner.agent) and the personal data associated with it.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to dinesh.yedla333@gmail.com with the subject line “Delete my account”.
  2. Send it from, or include, the Google email address you registered with so we can verify your identity.
  3. We will process your request and confirm by email once your account and data have been deleted.

What data is deleted

When you request account deletion, we delete:

  • Your account identity — email address, mobile phone number, and user ID.
  • Your notification subscription and push token.
  • Any profile and usage records we hold that are tied to your account.

What may be retained, and for how long

Your data is deleted from active systems within 30 days of a verified request. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 90 days. We may retain a minimal record only where required to comply with legal obligations.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can also ask us to delete specific data (for example, your phone number) without closing your account, by emailing dinesh.yedla333@gmail.com. You can turn off notifications at any time from within the app or your device settings.

Contact

Questions? Email dinesh.yedla333@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
