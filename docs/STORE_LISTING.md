# Play Store listing — draft copy

Paste into **Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing**.

Character limits are hard caps and Play rejects overruns, so **recount in the
console** before saving — counts below are approximate.

## Why change anything

Play search ranks on keyword relevance plus install/retention signals. At ~10
installs there are no install signals to trade on, so the listing text is the only
lever available. The current title, "AI Study Scan Agent", contains none of the
words a student actually types — nobody searches for "scan agent". They search
"NEET doubt solver", "homework help app", "JEE question answer".

## App name (30 char cap)

Current: `AI Study Scan Agent` (19) — descriptive of the tech, invisible to search.

| Option | Chars | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| `AI Doubt Solver: JEE NEET` | 25 | Strongest keywords; drops your brand |
| `Study Scan: AI Doubt Solver` | 27 | **Recommended** — keeps brand, adds intent |
| `AI Study Scan: Homework Help` | 28 | Broader, less exam-specific |

## After you publish

Store-listing changes go through review. Give it **24–48 hours**, then check
**Grow → Store performance** for impressions and conversion rate. Conversion
(store visits → installs) is the number that tells you whether the copy works.
Change one field at a time or you won't know what moved.

## What this cannot fix

The listing converts people who already reached your page. It does not bring them
there. At 10 installs, Play sends you almost no organic traffic, so the first
users have to come from somewhere you control — a student group, a college
WhatsApp group, a subreddit, a teacher. Listing work makes that traffic convert;
it does not replace it.
