Stuck on a question? Scan it and get a clear, step-by-step answer in seconds - in your own language.

AI Study Goal Agent is a study companion built for Indian students - school boards and competitive exams alike. Point your camera at any question from your textbook, worksheet or question paper, and get a worked solution you can actually learn from.

SCAN AND UNDERSTAND ANY QUESTION
Take a photo or upload a screenshot. The app reads the question - including maths symbols, powers and fractions - and explains it step by step: the concept, the method, each step, then the final answer. Works for Maths, Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

LEARN IN YOUR OWN LANGUAGE
Get the explanation in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali or Gujarati. Formulas and technical terms stay in English so you still recognise them in your exam. Change the language on the answer screen and the same question is explained again straight away - no need to scan it twice.

LISTEN TO ANY ANSWER
Tap Listen and the explanation is read aloud in the language you chose - useful when your eyes are tired, or when you are revising on the move.

BUILT FOR YOUR EXAM
Choose CBSE, JEE, NEET, EAMCET or UPSC and answers follow your syllabus. Or leave it on Auto and let the AI work it out.

KEY CONCEPT AND PRACTICE
After every answer you also get the concept behind it, plus practice questions to check you have really understood it - not just copied it.

PRACTICE QUESTIONS ON ANY TOPIC
Generate fresh practice questions with answers on any chapter you choose. Pick how many you want and revise at your own pace - useful for last-minute revision before a test.

AI STUDY PLANNER
Tell the app your exam, how many months you have and how many hours a day you can study. It builds a month-by-month plan with the topics to cover and a milestone for each month, so you always know what to study next.

DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS FOR UPSC
Opt in and receive current-affairs questions and answers drawn from the day's news, delivered as a notification at a time you choose - up to four times a day. Stay exam-ready without endlessly scrolling news apps.

DAILY STREAK
Solve at least one question a day and build your streak. A small nudge to keep going.

HISTORY
Every question you solve is saved, so you can revisit past doubts and revise before an exam.
