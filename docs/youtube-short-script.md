# YouTube Short / Instagram Reel — script 1
Format: 9:16 vertical, 22-25 seconds, screen recording + text overlays.
Shoot with your phone's built-in screen recorder. No editing skills needed.

## THE ONE RULE
Pick a question that is genuinely hard. A Class 6 sum impresses nobody.
Use a real JEE/NEET previous-year question - integration, rotational motion,
organic mechanism. The "wow" comes from the difficulty, not from your app.

## SHOT LIST

0:00-0:02  HOOK  (most people leave here - earn the next second)
  Visual : the hard question, filling the screen, in a textbook or on paper
  Text   : "Stuck on this at 1am?"
  Audio  : trending audio, low volume, or silence

0:02-0:05  THE PROMISE
  Visual : phone picks up, app opens, camera frames the question
  Text   : "Just scan it"

0:05-0:09  THE SCAN
  Visual : shutter tap, OCR text appears, "Interpreted as" line shows
  Text   : "It reads the maths correctly"
  (This is your actual edge - most scanner apps mangle powers and fractions.
   Let the viewer SEE t^3 come out right.)

0:09-0:18  THE PAYOFF  (the part people watch)
  Visual : scroll the step-by-step solution slowly, top to bottom
  Text   : "Full steps. Not just the answer."
  Do NOT speed this up. The satisfying part is watching real working appear.

0:18-0:22  THE TURN
  Visual : tap through to Home Work or Planner for one second each
  Text   : "Practice questions + a study plan too"

0:22-0:25  SOFT CTA
  Visual : app home screen
  Text   : "Free on Play Store - AI Study Goal Agent"
  Spoken : nothing. Let the text do it.

# Script 2 - "the one it got wrong" (builds trust, performs well)
Show the app solving 3 questions fast, and be honest that the 4th needed
checking. Counter-intuitively this earns more trust and more comments than
a flawless demo, because viewers assume flawless demos are faked.

Text overlay: "3 out of 4 correct. I'll take it at 1am."

## POSTING CADENCE
One Short per day for 30 days. Not one per week.
The algorithm needs volume to find your audience, and you need
reps to work out which hook lands. Most will get ~100 views.
One will get 50,000. That one is the whole point.
