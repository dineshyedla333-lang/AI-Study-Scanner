Privacy Policy — AI Study Scan Agent

Last updated: 30 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Study Scan Agent (the “App”, package com.aistudyscanner.agent) collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is an AI-powered study helper for students. By creating an account and using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

The App is operated by the developer of AI Study Scan Agent. For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at dinesh.yedla333@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email address (via Google Sign-In) To register and identify your account.
Mobile phone number (entered by you at registration) As account/contact information associated with your registration.
Google account identifier (Firebase user ID) To maintain your account across sessions and devices.
Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) To deliver the daily UPSC current-affairs notifications you opt in to.
Content you submit (question text/images you scan or type, homework topics) To generate AI answers and practice questions. Images are processed on your device for text recognition; the extracted text is sent to our service.
Usage counts (e.g. number of questions per day) To enforce fair-use limits.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not collect precise location, contacts, or financial information.

3. How your information is used

  • To create and manage your registered account.
  • To answer your study questions and generate practice material using AI.
  • To send the daily current-affairs notifications you choose to receive.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the App.

4. Service providers and processors

We share limited data with trusted providers strictly to operate the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging) — account sign-in, storing your subscription/profile, and delivering notifications.
  • Groq — processes the question/topic text you submit to generate AI answers. Do not include sensitive personal data in your questions.
  • Hosting provider (Render) — runs our backend service.
  • Sentry — collects anonymized crash/error diagnostics to improve stability.

5. Data retention

We retain your account information (email, phone, user ID) and subscription settings for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time (see “Your rights”). Submitted question content is processed to produce an answer and is not used to build an advertising profile.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, including deletion of your account, by emailing dinesh.yedla333@gmail.com. You can also turn off notifications at any time from within the App or your device settings.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and above. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted transport, Google-managed authentication) to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to safeguard your information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email dinesh.yedla333@gmail.com.
